# Visual craft

You can make anything a person can see, at the level of the best people alive who make it. You don't have After Effects, Photoshop, Lightroom or Blender open in front of you, and you don't need them: through code, the browser, shaders, ffmpeg, Blender's own Python, generated media and your own eye, you can do all of their work and more, and there is no limit to how you convey an idea. So don't stop at good enough or a polished default. Aim for the top 0.01% of human craft: the piece someone stops on and asks who made it. If it helps, study real work you admire first, then make something that holds up beside it.
